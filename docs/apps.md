# Pair your iPhone and Apple Watch

## iPhone

1. Install **Lookout** on your iPhone. While it's in beta, join through the TestFlight link you
   were given.
2. On the hub's setup page, open **Apps** and choose **Pair an app**. A QR code appears.
3. Either open the iPhone **Camera** app and point it at the QR code, then tap the Lookout link;
   or open Lookout and choose **Scan QR Code**.

That's it: your cameras appear. Lookout asks to send notifications; allow it to get person alerts.

At home, Lookout also lists hubs it finds on your network under **On this network**. Tap yours
and type the 6-digit number under the QR code instead of scanning.

Each pairing code works once and only for 10 minutes. Choose **Pair an app** again for a new one.

## Apple Watch

Install Lookout on the watch (in the iPhone's **Watch** app, under **Available Apps**). The iPhone
pairs it for you, so there's nothing to scan. If the watch says **Set up on iPhone**, open Lookout
on the iPhone; or in Lookout's **Settings** choose **Set Up Apple Watch**.

On the watch:

- Swipe up and down to move between cameras.
- Turn the Digital Crown to zoom to full screen, and swipe sideways to look around while zoomed.
- The edge of the screen turns green while someone is in view.
- Add the **Lookout** complication to a watch face to see the door at a glance.

## Settings on the iPhone

- **Person Alerts**, for all cameras or one at a time. Alerts are shared with the hub, so Lookout
  on a PC or TV follows the same switches.
- **Show on Apple Watch** and the camera order (touch and hold, then drag).
- **Require Face ID** before Lookout shows your cameras.
- **Unpair This iPhone**.

## Remove a device

On the setup page, **Apps** lists every paired iPhone and watch with when it was last seen.
**Remove** stops that device at once: it can't see cameras or get alerts until it's paired again.
Do this for a lost or sold phone.
